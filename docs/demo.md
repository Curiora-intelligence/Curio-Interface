# Four short demos

Use the same browser and `demo-sai` identity throughout. Start the backend and interface, verify the connection badge, and allow local inference time. Never restart the backend during an active run.

1. **A fresh chat, the same preferences.** Say “I prefer spicy chicken biryani and usually stay under ₹300.” Wait for the final response. Open Memories to show Food preference and Food budget. Click New Chat, then say “I'm hungry.” Follow with “Find something nearby.” Enable location in Settings for actual distance; without coordinates Curio must leave distance unknown. The catalogue and prices are fictional demo data.
2. **A shared view.** Choose Food, open Live, and select Service Discovery. Enable the camera and show a tap. Wait until Curio reports an observation. Ask “This is leaking. Find someone nearby who can fix it today.” The user supplies the leak assertion; a still frame alone does not prove a leak. Discover can also show the backend's plumber cards with same-day constraints and ranking reasons.
3. **Your kind of thing.** Say “I prefer minimal black products and my shopping budget is ₹2500.” Start a New Chat, select Shopping, and open Live. Show a product; after an observation, ask “Would I like this?” A remembered budget is not evidence of a product's price. Unseen price, brand, material, quality, and specifications must stay unknown. The backend includes a narrow guard against unsupported affordability claims found during real-model validation.
4. **Practice what’s next.** Say “I struggle with database indexing.” Click New Chat, select Career → Mock Interview, and ask “Mock interview me for a backend internship.” In Live's Mock Interview mode, start a communication sample, dictate/type an answer, finish the sample, then Send. Display pace, pauses, speaking ratio, and volume variation. Visible gaze/head/posture are sparse observations, not psychological measurements.

Expand “How Curio handled this” to show confirmed stages and actual tool execution. Do not present a memory-retrieval stage as evidence of a particular memory being used; the Memories panel shows what is saved, and the final answer shows what Curio chose to mention.
